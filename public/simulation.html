How it works

  • Three hospitals with the same demand profile as the demo. One over-orders for its usage, one runs lean.
  • Each morning, sites order up to a par level of their own demand. Units arrive with 3 to 4 days of shelf life. Sites don't stock AB platelets.
  • Demand per blood group is random (Poisson). Both policies see exactly the same demand and deliveries, so differences come from the rule alone.
  • Patients get platelets in the order set by the SNBTS Policy for Platelet Selection: identical group first, then compatible groups. Group O never goes to non-O patients, and RhD-positive never goes to RhD-negative patients.
  • With the rule, the same engine the live app uses runs every morning and moves units projected to expire unused to a site that will use them.
  • Each run has a 5-day warm-up, then 30 counted days. Results are averaged over 20 runs.

Published programs that also changed ordering and practice reported about 20% fewer outdates at Emory (Zhang et al., Operations Research 2023) and waste falling from 17% to 7.8% in Adelaide (BloodMove). This simulation tests transfers alone.
